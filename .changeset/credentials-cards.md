Saved cards through the AgentCard vault. `agent credentials cards add` returns a one-time link where the user saves a card behind their passkey, `cards wait` waits until it's stored, and `credentials cards` lists brand, last four digits, and expiry (never the number). The dashboard's Wallet tab gets a "Connect a card securely" panel that opens the same page and shows connected cards.
